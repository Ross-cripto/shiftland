Know what's open

Work across displays

Each display shows its own spaces. The MacBook bar fits next to the notch.

Keep your setup yours

Name your spaces, choose which display they appear on, and set the shortcuts you want to use.

Try it on your Mac.

Shiftland runs on macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Open the download and drag Shiftland to Applications.

The first time you open it, macOS asks for your approval. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
