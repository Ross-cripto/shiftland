Your spaces,
at a glance.

The bar follows your display and shows the apps in each workspace. Choose a number to try a coding session, a research space, or a planning desk. Use the arrow keys when the picker is focused.

Know what's open

Find spaces by number and app icon, and see which one is active.

Work across displays

Each display shows its own spaces. The MacBook bar fits next to the notch.

Keep your setup yours

Edit names, display assignments, and shortcuts. Shiftland backs up your OmniWM settings when you save.

Try it on your Mac.

Build with Xcode's command-line tools. Install OmniWM and enable IPC to connect the two apps.

Verified with macOS 15.5 and OmniWM 0.5.1.
