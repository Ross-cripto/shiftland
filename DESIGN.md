# Shiftland

The workspace bar follows the numeric layout from the original SketchyBar pilot.

On the external monitor, center the workspace group in the macOS menu bar. On the MacBook, place it immediately left of the notch. Show the workspaces assigned by OmniWM to that display. Respect the operating system's menu controls outside the group.

Use the existing slate capsule, 48-point slots, 13-point Helvetica Neue Medium, 16-point native app icons and blue focus treatment. Empty spaces retain their number. Two applications share an icon slot, with each contributing half of its icon. Tooltips list all application names. Switching and focus feedback are immediate.

Settings use macOS controls and group the work into Espacios, Pantallas and Atajos. Show where settings come from, make saving explicit and report errors next to the save controls.

## App identity

Shiftland's mark is a single ivory hare in a forward leap, with two diagonally clipped ears and a small wedge tail. The app icon centers the hare on a deep aubergine tile. The menu bar uses the same profile as a monochrome template at 18 × 16 points. The workspace controls retain their cool slate and blue focus palette.
